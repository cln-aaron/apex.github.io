// Quick Answers

Before You Ask.

Got questions? We figured. Here are the ones everyone asks.

Is this free?

Yes. Completely free. No hidden fees, no upsell, no catch. This bootcamp is funded by our organising partners to invest in Singapore's next generation.

Do I need experience?

Zero. Whether you're from ITE, poly, JC, or uni, and whether you study tech, arts, business, or anything else, the tracks are designed for beginners. Curiosity is the only prerequisite.

Where is it?

TrendAI Office, Singapore. The exact address will be shared with confirmed participants via email.

Can anyone from overseas take part?

This bootcamp is only open to those currently residing in Singapore. You must be a full-time student at a Singapore institution to be eligible.

When is it?

13 and 14 June 2026, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on both days. It's a Friday and Saturday.

Do I get a certificate?

Every participant receives a digital certificate of completion. Top teams get priority access to partner internship listings, alumni community, and mentorship continuation.

Can I switch tracks during the bootcamp?

You pick one primary track, but cross-track challenges on both days mean you'll collaborate with every track. The collisions are the point.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

We review applications in waves. You'll receive a confirmation email if you're selected, with all the details you need to prepare.

Who is this for?

Full-time students aged 16 to 30, currently enrolled in any Singapore institution: secondary school, JC, ITE, polytechnic, or university. Any course, any background.

Still have questions? Reach out and we'll get back to you.
