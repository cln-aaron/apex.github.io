Track 03

Cybersecurity & Bug Bounty

Cybersecurity has a global shortage of 4M+ professionals. Unlike most careers, ethical hacking lets you start earning before you have a job — via bug bounties that pay anyone who finds real flaws.

Day 1 · How Hackers Think

  • The hacker mindset: curiosity, persistence, lateral thinking
  • OSINT lab: what can you find using only public info?
  • Social engineering & phishing: how attackers manipulate people
  • AI in cybersecurity — used by attackers AND defenders

Day 2 · Capture the Flag

  • Beginner-friendly CTF in a sandboxed environment
  • Tiered: Rookie · Challenger · Elite
  • 5 scenario tracks to choose from (below)
  • No real systems at risk — safe to fail, safe to learn
